Privacy Policy

Giovanni Sanchez · last updated November 13, 2026.

This policy explains what information Giovanni Sanchez ("Giovanni Sanchez", "we") collects from business owners and visitors to https://doctorg1998.github.io/local-websites, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Business information you give us to build your site: business name, address, hours, menu, prices, photos, logo, social links, and the name, email and phone of the person we work with.
  • Billing information is collected and stored by our payment processor, Stripe. We see your name, email, the last 4 digits of your card, and payment status, never your full card or bank number. If you pay by Zelle, we see only what your bank shows us: your name and the amount.
  • Messages you send us by email or text.
  • Our website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

How we use it

  • To build, host and update your website.
  • To bill you, send receipts, and contact you about your account.
  • To answer your questions.

Information you ask us to put on your website (your hours, menu, address, public phone number) becomes public on your site.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it with the services we need to run your website and billing: Stripe (payments), GitHub and Cloudflare (hosting), your domain registrar, and Google (email and documents). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your plan is active, and up to 12 months after you cancel (so we can return your content or reactivate your site), unless tax or legal rules require us to keep billing records longer. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

Email giovannisanchez.website@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete your information, or to stop non-essential messages.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, email active clients.
