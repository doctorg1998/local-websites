Website Services Agreement

Also the Terms of Service for every purchase from Giovanni Sanchez. Last updated November 09, 2026.

This Agreement is between Giovanni Sanchez, a sole proprietor in Miami-Dade County, Florida ("we", "us"), and the business that signs up for a website plan ("you", the "Client"). You accept this Agreement when you check the terms box at checkout, sign it, or pay any invoice that refers to it.

1. What you get

The Website Plan includes:

  • A one-page website made for phones, tablets and computers. It can show your menu or services, hours, location and map, contact buttons, photos, and links to your social media and ordering apps.
  • Setup: we build the site from the information and photos you give us, and you get one round of changes before launch.
  • Hosting for as long as your plan is active, a security certificate (https), and connecting your domain name.
  • Unlimited small updates to menu items, prices, hours, photos, specials and text. We make them within 2 business days of your request. You can send requests by email or text.
  • One check-in per month asking if anything needs updating.
  • Linking your Google Business Profile to your site and keeping its hours current, if you add us as a manager. A full profile tune-up (photos, categories, description) is an optional extra for $49, one time.
  • Accessibility: we build sites following WCAG 2.1 AA practices, such as menus as real text (not images or PDFs), descriptions for images, and readable color contrast. We do not guarantee that a site meets any accessibility law or standard.

2. What is not included

Unless we agree in writing, the plan does not include: online ordering, booking or payment systems (we can link to the ones you already use); logo or brand design; professional photography; copywriting beyond short descriptions; paid ads, search ranking guarantees, or social media management; multi-page sites; or email accounts. We can quote these separately.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information (menu, prices, hours, address, allergens) and tell us when it changes. You are responsible for what your site says about your products and prices.
  • Only give us photos, logos and text that you own or have permission to use. If someone claims that a photo, logo or text you gave us infringes their rights, you agree to cover the reasonable costs of that claim.
  • Name one person who can approve the site and request changes.

4. Price and payment

  • Down payment: $99 ($49 for founding clients), paid at signup. It covers setup, the first year of your domain name (if you need a new one), and payment processing.
  • Monthly plan: $49 per month. The first monthly charge happens on the day your site launches, and the same day each month after that.
  • Founding clients: our first 10 clients pay a $49 down payment and $39 per month for their first 12 months. After that, the regular $49 monthly price applies, and we will remind them by email at least 30 days before it changes. In return, the founding client agrees to leave an honest Google review and lets us show their site as an example of our work.
  • Payments are charged automatically to the card or bank account you give at checkout, through our payment processor (Stripe). We never see or store your full card number. If we agree, you can instead pay by Zelle or another method we agree on, against an invoice we email you each month; invoices are due within 7 days.
  • Domain renewals after the first year are included in your monthly plan while it is active.
  • We will give you at least 30 days' notice by email before changing your monthly price. A founding client's $39 price is guaranteed for their first 12 months.

5. Late or failed payments

If a card payment fails, our processor will retry it and email you. If an invoice is not paid by its due date, we will remind you by email. If it is still unpaid 14 days after the due date, we may pause your site (it shows a simple "back soon" page) until the balance is paid. A site unpaid for 60 days may be cancelled under section 7.

6. 30-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy, ask for a refund within 30 days after your site launches and we will refund the down payment and your first monthly payment. The only thing not refunded is a domain name already bought for you, which stays registered to you and is yours to keep. After that 30-day window, payments are not refundable. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Cancelling

  • The plan is month to month. There is no long-term contract.
  • You can cancel anytime in the billing portal linked in your receipts, or by emailing giovannisanchez.website@gmail.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for, and your site stays online until then. No further charges are made.
  • We can end the plan with 30 days' written notice, or right away if you break this Agreement, do not pay for 60 days, or ask us to publish anything illegal, harmful or misleading. If we end the plan without cause, we refund any unused prepaid time.

8. Who owns what

  • Your domain name is yours. We register it in your name (or your business name). If you leave, we help you move it at no charge. A newly registered domain cannot be moved to another registrar for 60 days after registration (an industry rule), so a move can start after that.
  • Your content is yours: your menu, text, photos, logo and business information.
  • The website design and code are owned by us. While your plan is active you have a license to use them for your business. If you cancel, you can buy a full copy of your site files for a one-time fee of $300 (waived after 12 paid months), and then you may use and host that copy anywhere.
  • We show your site in our portfolio only with your permission (founding clients give this permission as part of their discount).

9. Hosting and availability

We host your site with reputable providers and aim to keep it online at all times, but we cannot guarantee it will never be unavailable, slow, or affected by outages at our providers, your domain registrar or the internet. We will fix problems we control as quickly as reasonably possible.
