Questions

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. Send photos and your menu, then text or email any changes. I handle everything else.

Do you work with food trucks?

Yes. Food trucks get the same plan: your menu, hours, the spots where you usually park, and a WhatsApp button for orders.

What if I already have a website or a domain?

I can rebuild it and connect the domain you already own.

Can customers order online?

Your site links to the ordering apps you already use, like DoorDash, Uber Eats, Toast, Square or WhatsApp.

Do you have a referral deal?

Yes. Send me a business that signs up, and you get a free month after their first monthly payment clears.

Can you help with my Google Maps listing?

Yes. Linking it to your site and keeping your hours right is included. A full tune-up (photos, categories, description) is $49 one time.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel any time by text or email. Your site stays online until the month you paid for ends, and your domain stays yours. I help you move it if you want. See the refund & cancellation policy.
