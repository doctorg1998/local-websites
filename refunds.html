Refund & Cancellation Policy

Giovanni Sanchez · last updated November 09, 2026.

The short version

  • No long-term contract. Month to month, cancel anytime.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee after your site launches.
  • Your domain stays yours, even if you leave.

30-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with your website, email giovannisanchez.website@gmail.com within 30 days after your site goes live. We will refund your $49 down payment (minus the domain fee of about $12, if we already bought a domain for you) and your first monthly payment, no questions asked.

The one exception: if we bought a domain name for you, its registration fee (about $12) is not refunded, because that domain is registered to you and you keep it.

If you change your mind before we start building, we refund the whole down payment.

Refunds go back the same way you paid (card, bank account or Zelle). Card refunds can take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

After the first 30 days

Monthly payments are not refundable once the month has started, but you can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again.

How to cancel

  1. Email giovannisanchez.website@gmail.com and say "please cancel". If you pay by card, you can also click "Manage subscription" in any payment receipt email.
  2. Your site stays online until the end of the month you already paid for.
  3. We send you a confirmation by email.

When you leave

  • Domain: we help you transfer it to any provider you choose, for free.
  • Your content (menu, photos, text) is yours. We send it back on request.
  • Site files: you can buy a complete copy of your website for $300 (free after 12 paid months) and host it anywhere.

Payment problems

If a payment fails, we retry it and email you. If it stays unpaid for 14 days, the site is paused until it is paid. Nothing is deleted while paused.

Questions? giovannisanchez.website@gmail.com
